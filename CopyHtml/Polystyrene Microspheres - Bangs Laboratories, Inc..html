Polystyrene Microspheres

Polystyrene microspheres are a flexible platform for applications in diagnostics and bioseparations. They may be coated with recognition molecules, such as antibodies, antigens, peptides, or nucleic acid probes via adsorption. We offer surface modified microspheres with carboxyl groups, primary amine groups, and click chemistry reactive groups for a more stable covalent attachment. Polystyrene microspheres are also available with surface coatings of streptavidin, Protein A or G, and other binding proteins for affinity purification or depletion applications. Unmodified polymer spheres also find extensive use as standards for set-up and calibration of particle/cell counting or flow cytometry instruments. Plain polystyrene microspheres can also be loaded with hydrophobic dyes and other compounds for tracking cellular uptake pathways or drug targeting or distribution efficiency.

Product points:

  • Available Diameters: ~20nm-90µm
  • Size CVs of 5-10%
  • 10% solids suspension (100mg/mL)
